About the portal

A more responsive campus, together

CampusCare provides a clear, organised channel for reporting campus maintenance concerns and following their progress.

Our purpose

Make it easier for students to report maintenance issues, help campus teams understand what needs attention, and provide visibility into each request. From a leaking tap to a classroom facility issue, every report should be easy to record and track.

Need help?

Frequently asked questions

A few things to know about using the CampusCare demo.

How do I submit a complaint?

Go to the Campus complaint centre, complete the required fields and select Submit complaint. A unique complaint ID will be generated.

Can I check the complaint status?

Yes. The complaint records section shows the current status. Use the search field or status filter to find a request.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens, including the navigation, forms and complaint cards.

Are complaints shared with everyone?

This version is a front-end demonstration. Records are saved in the current browser only and are not shared between devices or users.
